Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026

This policy explains what information GrilledCoin ("we", "the app") collects when you use the website, desktop app, or installed mobile app, and how it's used.

Information we collect

  • Account data: username, email address, hashed password, and the Patreon username you provide at sign-up.
  • Google Sign-In (optional): if you sign in with Google, we receive your name, email, and profile picture from Google to create or match your account.
  • Gameplay data: your virtual chip balance, bet history, and game results, used to operate the games and provably-fair verification.
  • Usage analytics: we use Google Analytics to understand aggregate usage (pages visited, session length). This data is anonymized and not tied to your account by us.
  • Advertising: on some pages we display ads via Google AdSense, which may use cookies to personalize ads. You can opt out of personalized advertising through Google Ads Settings.

How we use this information

  • To create and secure your account and verify your Patreon subscription tier.
  • To operate the games, track your virtual balance, and display leaderboards/history.
  • To understand how the app is used so we can improve it.
  • To serve ads that help support the service (where shown).

What we don't do

  • We don't sell your personal information.
  • GrilledCoin uses virtual chips only — we never process real-money wagers, and chips have no cash value.

Data retention & deletion

Account and gameplay data is kept for as long as your account is active. You can request deletion of your account and associated data at any time by contacting us at the address below.

Children's privacy

GrilledCoin requires an active Patreon subscription to access and is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 18.

Contact

Questions about this policy? Reach out via the Patreon page linked on our sign-up screen.
